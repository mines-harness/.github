<h1 align="center">mines</h1>

<p align="center">Your coding agents. Your subscriptions. Your computers.</p>

<p align="center">
  <a href="https://github.com/mines-harness/macos">Explore the source</a> ·
  <a href="https://github.com/mines-harness/macos/releases">Releases</a> ·
  <a href="https://github.com/mines-harness/macos/tree/main/docs">Read the docs</a>
</p>

Mines brings coding agents into one workspace across desktop, web, and mobile. Run agents through their native provider tools, keep projects on their owning host, and connect from another approved device.

![Mines web composer with separate model, device, and effort controls](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mines-harness/macos/main/assets/showcase/web-composer.png)

Native mobile preview captures from a simulator and emulator, with no live host or provider session.

- Organize mines, projects, groups, and pinned work in one library.
- Compare models or work with a group of existing agents.
- Use your own provider accounts and inspect supported imported histories.
- Connect computers and phones through authenticated direct device connections.
